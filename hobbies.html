Hobbies

Curiosity does not stop at code.

I like hobbies that reward patience, attention, and a little experimentation. Fountain pens are my favorite example.

The Fountain Pen Habit

Writing becomes more intentional.

Fountain pens make an ordinary note feel more personal. I enjoy the mechanical side of them, from a nib's feel on paper to the way an ink changes character as it dries.

Tinkering is part of the fun: keeping a pen clean, trying a different ink, and learning what makes a pen comfortable for everyday writing. It is a calm contrast to a busy day at a screen.
