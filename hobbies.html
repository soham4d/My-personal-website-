Hobbies

I like hobbies that reward patience, attention, and a little experimentation.

Fountain pens are my favorite example of this way of thinking.

The Fountain Pen Habit

A calm contrast to a busy day at a screen.

Fountain pens make an ordinary note feel more personal. I enjoy the mechanical side of them, from a nib's feel on paper to the way an ink changes character as it dries.

Tinkering is part of the fun: keeping a pen clean, trying a different ink, and learning what makes a pen comfortable for everyday writing.

Nib feel

Every nib has a different voice, from a smooth daily writer to one with a little feedback.
